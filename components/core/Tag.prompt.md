Square keyword chip — matières on an avocate's fiche, or a topic marker.

```jsx
<Tag onClick={go}>Droit des étrangers</Tag>
<Tag>Droit du roulage</Tag>
```

Notes: 48px min height, square corners. With `onClick` it inverts on hover like every other clickable frame; without one it is a plain non-interactive label. `selected` renders it filled brun profond.
