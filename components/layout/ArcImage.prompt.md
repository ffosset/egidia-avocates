Every photograph on an Egidia surface goes through this: the shield's arc, cut into one corner.

```jsx
<ArcImage src="/assets/photos/equipe.jpg" alt="Les sept avocates du cabinet" corner="bottom-left" />
<ArcImage ratio="3 / 4" legend="Portrait à fournir" />
```

Notes: the arc is the ONLY curve in the system and it belongs to the mark, not to the interface — never apply a radius to a frame, a button or a card to "match" it. Default arc is bottom-left, as on the home hero. Without `src` you get an honest dashed placeholder, which is what ships until the cabinet supplies photography.
